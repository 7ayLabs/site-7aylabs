This is the largest Vizzor release so far. Three things stand out.

## Communities

Groups can now create a community inside Vizzor, with rooms, channels, live chat and their own rules. Anyone in a room can mention Vizzor and get an answer right there, including a prediction card when the question calls for one. Communities can also track wallets together and run prize pools for their members.

## Skills 2.0

A skill is a ready-made way of analysing a market. There is now a directory of 30 of them, a lab where you combine several into one, and the option to create and share your own. Every answer shows which skill produced it and why.

## A dashboard about you

The dashboard used to show the market. Now it shows your results: the profit and loss of your own graded calls over time, your ranking, and a portfolio built from the wallets you link.

## Also in this release

- A rebuilt command palette for getting anywhere quickly.
- A simpler layout for pools and daily challenges.
- Better behaviour when Vizzor is installed as an app on a phone.
