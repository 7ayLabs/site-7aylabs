The previous version of this site described an earlier project. It no longer matched what the team works on every day, so we replaced it.

## What changed

- **One subject.** The site is about 7ayLabs and its product, Vizzor. Everything else is gone.
- **The same look as Vizzor.** The company site and the product now share one design language, so moving between them feels like staying in the same place.
- **Three languages.** English, Spanish and French, the same three Vizzor speaks.
- **Less data.** No analytics and no tracking cookies. The only things we store are the email you give us for updates and the messages you send.
