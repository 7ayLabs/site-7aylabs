The previous version of this site described an earlier project. It no longer matched what the team works on every day, so we replaced it.

## What changed

- **The lab comes first.** The home page walks through the four instruments on the bench, an example run from raw block to answer, and the rules a release has to clear.
- **Vizzor has its own page.** It is Release 01, and its full story lives on [its page](/vizzor) instead of taking over the home page.
- **Your language, picked for you.** English, Spanish or French, chosen from where you are and what your browser asks for. The switch in the header overrides it, and the site remembers.
- **Less data.** No analytics and no tracking cookies. We store the email you give us for updates, the messages you send, and one cookie holding your language.
